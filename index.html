About Me

Hi, I'm Deendayal

I am an intraday trader focused on price action, market structure, and risk-managed trading. My goal is to trade with discipline, patience, and a clear process instead of emotions.

I believe trading is not about predicting every market move. It is about preparation, execution, and protecting capital first.

How I Trade

01. Pre-Market Planning

Before market open, I identify key levels, market sentiment, and potential trading opportunities.

02. Price Action Focus

I rely on clean charts, support & resistance, and volume confirmation rather than excessive indicators.

03. Risk Management

Every trade has a predefined stop loss, position size, and risk-reward ratio.

04. Review & Improve

I maintain a trading journal and review trades regularly to improve consistency.
