About Me

Hi, I'm Deendayal

I am an intraday trader focused on price action, market structure, and risk-managed trading. My goal is to trade with discipline, patience, and a clear process instead of emotions.

I believe trading is not about predicting every market move. It is about preparation, execution, and protecting capital first.

My Trading Approach
